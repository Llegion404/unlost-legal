Privacy Policy

Unlost · Effective date: 30 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to Unlost (the “app”), developed by the Unlost developer (“we”, “us”).

Summary

Unlost is a local notification archive. It uses Android notification listener access to save notification events on your device so you can browse, search, delete, restore, back up, and export them.

The app does not include the internet permission and does not upload, sell, or share your notification history with us or with third parties.

Data the app can access

When you enable notification access in Android settings, Unlost can read notifications posted by other apps. Saved records can include:

  • The source app package name and app label.
  • Notification title, text, ticker text, channel, category, group, actions, metadata, and a simplified raw notification snapshot.
  • Posted and removed event timestamps.
  • Starred state, tags, and notes you add yourself.
  • Export and backup files you explicitly create or restore through Android system file pickers.

Notification content can contain personal or sensitive information depending on what other apps put in notifications.

How data is used

Unlost uses saved notification data only to provide local app features:

  • Browsing, searching, filtering, and viewing notification history.
  • Deleting individual events or all events from a source app.
  • Starring, tagging, and annotating notifications you choose to keep.
  • Optional local retention cleanup.
  • Optional local database encryption.
  • Optional local backups to a folder you choose.
  • User-initiated exports to JSON, CSV, TXT, PDF, or Markdown files.
  • Optional statistics computed from your own local history.

Storage and sharing

Notification history is stored locally on your device in the app database. Backups and exports are written only to locations you choose through Android system file pickers.

The app does not transmit saved notification data over a network. If you share an exported or backed-up file with another app or person, that sharing is controlled by you and by the receiving app or service.

Purchases

Unlost offers an optional one-time in-app purchase that unlocks additional features. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details, and the app has no server that receives purchase or usage data.

Retention and deletion

You can delete saved notifications in the app. You can also configure automatic retention cleanup for older notifications. Uninstalling the app removes the app database from the device, but files you exported or backed up outside the app may remain until you delete them.

Security

Unlost disables Android cloud backup for its app data. The app also offers optional local database encryption and an optional secure screen setting that blocks screenshots and hides app content in the recents preview.

Children

Unlost is not designed for children and is not directed at children.
